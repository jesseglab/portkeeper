## Design Context

### Users
Developers use Portkeeper on macOS to manage development projects, ports, and service consoles while coding. Preserve existing AI diagnostics, service controls, and project handoff workflows.

### Brand Personality
Technical, focused, fluid. Use terminal-like black backgrounds and green text, with exceptionally smooth, liquid-like morphing, easing, and transitions.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the black-and-green terminal direction. Porty is a small green blob with large black pill eyes and a friendly smile, replacing the lighthouse. Use native layers for subtle, status-driven expressions and occasional blinks. Motion grows from the hardware notch and right screen edge. Prefer continuous curves and controlled settling over bouncy effects. Black remains opaque to blend into the camera housing.

### Design Principles
- Treat expansion, surface curvature, content reveal, and dismissal as one coordinated motion.
- Preserve velocity when a hover reverses; never restart an animation from a stale endpoint.
- Keep terminal text crisp: animate the surface mask and subtle content transforms, not text layout on every frame.
- Respect Reduce Motion and keep click, keyboard, and service behavior predictable.
- Use a display-synchronized driver only during transitions..
